## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information

When you register and use Athlofit, we may collect the following personal information:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, password (stored in encrypted/hashed form), date of birth, age, gender
- **Profile Information:** Profile photo/avatar, phone number, blood type
- **Body Metrics:** Height, weight, BMI records
- **Delivery Addresses:** Full name, phone number, street address, city, state, zip code, country (for in-app shop orders)

### 2.2 Health & Fitness Data

Athlofit collects health and fitness data that you provide or that is synced from your device:

- **Activity Data:** Daily step count, distance walked (km), calories burned (kcal), active minutes
- **Vitals:** Heart rate (average, minimum, maximum), blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), blood glucose levels (mmol/L)
- **Body Composition:** Weight, height, BMI calculations and category history
- **Hydration:** Daily water intake (ml)
- **Sleep:** Sleep duration (hours)
- **Nutrition:** Meal logs (food name, calories, protein, carbs, fat, quantity), dietary preferences (vegetarian/non-vegetarian/vegan), dietary goals, calorie targets, favourite foods

### 2.3 Gamification & Activity Data

- Coin balance and transaction history
- Daily streak and badge information
- Achievement progress and claim records
- Leaderboard rankings
- Referral codes and referral history

### 2.4 Device & Technical Information

- **Device Platform:** iOS or Android
- **Push Notification Token:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token
- **Session Data:** IP address, user-agent string (collected during authentication)
- **Search Activity:** Food catalog search queries and clicked items

### 2.5 Third-Party Authentication

If you sign in using Google:
- Google account ID
- Email address
- Name (given name, family name)
- Profile photo URL
- Granted OAuth scopes

We use the information we collect to:

- **Provide Core Services:** Track your daily steps, health metrics, nutrition, and fitness progress
- **Gamification:** Calculate coin rewards, manage streaks, award badges, and maintain leaderboard rankings
- **In-App Shop:** Process orders using virtual coins, manage delivery addresses, and handle order fulfillment

- **Account Security:** Authenticate your identity, manage sessions, and protect against unauthorised access
- **Customer Support:** Respond to support requests you submit through the App

## 5. Data Storage & Security

### 5.1 Storage

Your data is stored on secure MongoDB database servers. Authentication tokens are encrypted and automatically expire. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a salt factor of 12 and are never stored in plain text.

### 5.2 Security Measures

We implement the following security measures:

- Password hashing (bcrypt)
- JWT-based authentication with token rotation
- Refresh token expiration and revocation
- Rate limiting on API endpoints
- Security headers (Helmet)
- HTTPS encryption for data in transit
- Input validation and sanitisation

### 5.3 Data Retention

- **Health Data:** Retained as long as your account is active
- **Session Tokens:** Refresh tokens automatically expire and are deleted
- **Notifications:** Limited to the most recent 200 per user
- **Transaction History:** Retained for your records as long as your account is active
- **Claim History:** Limited to the most recent 50 entries
